English

T Jumping Car ("the game") is made by Leong Chee Fook. This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any account. We have no servers and we do not receive any of your data.

Information stored on your device

Your best score and sound setting are saved only on your device. Deleting the game deletes them.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device type and how you interact with ads. This is handled by Google under its own policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your phone settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: coffeelove2011@gmail.com
